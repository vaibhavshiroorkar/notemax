Everything a long piece of writing needs

Write

  • Headings, lists, to-dos, quotes, code, pictures and video. Type / for any block.
  • Word-like fonts, colors and spacing. Paste from Word or Google Docs and keep the look.
  • [[Links]] between notes that survive renames, with backlinks on every page.
  • Drawing boards, voice typing, and code pages that run Python or JavaScript.

Shape it into a book

  • Books with chapters, each holding numbered subtopics.
  • Read or edit the whole book on one page, or lay a page out as A4 or Letter sheets with page numbers.
  • Download a book as Word, PDF or EPUB.
  • Post notes and books to the community, and follow other writers.

Keep it yours

  • Saved on your device as you type. Startup and typing are tested with 5,000 notes.
  • Optional sync through your own Google Drive, locked with a passphrase so Google can't read it.
  • Version history for every note and a trash you can restore from.
  • Download everything as plain Markdown whenever you like.

Write with others

  • Share a note by link and write in it together, live and end-to-end encrypted.
  • Share a whole workspace by email or link, as viewer, editor or admin.
  • Separate workspaces for separate parts of your life, each one lockable.

Start writing

Open Notemax in this browser, or install it on Windows or Android. Your notes stay on the device you write them on until you turn on sync.
