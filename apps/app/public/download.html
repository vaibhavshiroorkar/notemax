Get Notemax

Notemax works in your browser. It also comes as an app, so it opens on its own and keeps working with no internet.

For this device

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

  • Your pages live in a file on your PC
  • Sits in the tray, and Ctrl+Alt+N takes a quick note from anywhere
  • Updates itself when a new version is out
Download for Windows

Finding the newest version...

Windows says the app is from an unknown publisher

The installer is not signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows asks first. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Updates after that are checked against Notemax's own signature.

For this device

Android

Chrome, Edge or Samsung Internet

  • Opens from your home screen like any app
  • Works offline, with your pages on the phone
  • Gets each new version on its own

To install it:

  1. Open notemax.pages.dev in Chrome on your phone.
  2. Tap the menu (the three dots at the top right).
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

Installed. Open Notemax from your home screen.
